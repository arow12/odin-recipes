This is chicken Salad Recipe Page

    Ingredients
  • 2 boneless chicken breast halves, cooked
  • ¼ cup creamy salad dressing (such as Miracle Whip)
  • 4 tablespoons coleslaw dressing
  • 1 stalk celery, chopped
  • ¼ onion, chopped
  • salt and pepper to taste
    Steps
  1. Combine cooked chicken, creamy salad dressing, coleslaw dressing, celery, onion, salt, and pepper in a food processor
  2. Pulse until ingredients are chopped and blended to desired consistency
